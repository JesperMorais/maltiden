## Summary vs. previously claimed targets

Two chunks exceed 100 KB raw:

- `motion-CTbkWtDD.js` — **123.2 KB raw** (40.9 KB gzip). Roughly matches the
  ~124 KB target.

The previously claimed reductions hold within rounding (±1 KB).

## Splitting candidates for chunks > 100 KB

- **motion-CTbkWtDD.js (123 KB)** — `motion-v` is currently bundled eagerly.
  Candidate: async-import `motion-v` only inside views that actually use rich
  animation (e.g. `LandingView`, `OnboardingView`, `DashboardView`) via dynamic
  `import('motion-v')`, leaving auth/utility views (`LoginView`,
  `ForgotPasswordView`, `ResetPasswordView`, `AboutView`) free of the motion
  runtime on first load.
- **vue-vendor-DJzSIJSr.js (109 KB)** — already a vendor split of Vue + Router
  + Pinia. Further splitting risks waterfalls on initial paint; leave as-is
  unless the landing view's TTI regresses. (Not recommended as the next
  target.)

Top candidate to pursue next: **motion-v on-demand import from non-landing
views.** Do NOT implement in this task.
