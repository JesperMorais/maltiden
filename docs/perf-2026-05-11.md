## Chunks Still > 100 KB — Split Candidates

### `motion-BokbazRX.js` (126 KB)

`motion-v` is imported by five components: `RollingCounter.vue`, `RotatingText.vue`, `MenuDayCard.vue`, `ToastNotification.vue`, and `FeedbackWidget.vue`. Only `MenuDayCard` is on the critical path for authenticated routes; the `vue-bits` components (`RollingCounter`, `RotatingText`) are used exclusively in `LandingView` and `OnboardingView`. Wrapping those two animation components in `defineAsyncComponent` would allow the motion chunk to be deferred until those routes are visited, keeping it out of the initial bundle entirely.

### `vue-vendor-CAnRlPs5.js` (111 KB)

Contains `vue`, `vue-router`, and `pinia` — the core framework trinity. Splitting any of these off the critical path would require lazy-initialising the router or store, which introduces more risk than benefit. This chunk is effectively irreducible unless the team considers dropping one of the three libraries. Not a split candidate at current scale.

## Conclusion

Both `motion` and `vue-vendor` remain above 100 KB; the baseline reduction from #184 has not regressed dramatically but has not held exactly — **further work warranted** on `motion-v` deferral (estimated ~126 KB savings off the critical-path parse) and a review of the 6 KB `DashboardView` growth since the last snapshot.
